The single text-entry primitive. Renders `input`, `textarea` or `select`.

```jsx
<Input placeholder="e.g., Go Ape Ship!" />
<Input as="textarea" placeholder="Tell players what makes your game worth their time…" />
<Input as="select"><option>App Store</option></Input>
```

6px radius, 9×12 padding, background `--bg` (darker than the panel it sits on).
