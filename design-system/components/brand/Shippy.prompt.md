The mascot. Warm, never loud — one Shippy per page at most, and never as decoration in a dense screen.

He stands on his own — full body, front-facing, floating. Do not crop him to a peeking pose.

\`glow\` is a soft purple bloom that fades fully to transparent; leave it off on a busy surface.

**The legacy Shippy** — the flatter periwinkle \`#727BF0\` octopus with a rectangular head — is still what ships on the live splash page. Those five layers live in \`assets/brand/legacy/\` and are used only by \`ui_kits/marketing/\` and \`templates/marketing-page/\`, where the body sits behind the card pill and the tentacles drape in front. Use this component everywhere else.
